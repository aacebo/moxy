# AI-Assisted Contributions

AI tools may be used to assist with contributions to Moxy. Contributors are
responsible for everything they submit, whether or not an AI tool was used.

## Human Responsibility

Before opening a pull request, contributors must review, test, and understand
every proposed change. Contributors must be able to explain the purpose,
design, and tradeoffs of their changes and make requested revisions without
relying on an AI tool to do so.

Autonomous agents must not submit or interact with the repository on a
contributor's behalf. An agent may assist a human contributor, but a human must
select, review, test, and submit each change.

## Scope And Reviewability

Keep pull requests small and focused. Split unrelated changes into separate
pull requests. Substantial changes to public APIs, parsing behavior,
architecture, CI, or project-wide behavior must be discussed with maintainers
before implementation.

Pull requests must include sufficient human-written context to explain the
problem being solved, the approach taken, and how the change was tested. Do not
use AI-generated text for pull request descriptions or replies to maintainer
questions. AI may be used for translation or accessibility assistance when the
contributor verifies that the submitted text expresses their own meaning.

## Maintainer Discretion

Maintainers may close contributions that are too broad to review, insufficiently
understood or tested by their author, or otherwise do not follow this policy.
